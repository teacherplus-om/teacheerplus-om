Interactive classroom activities guide

A rich collection of suggested activities from the the Teacher’s Book (Games bank) and the Class Book, organised and ready to use in the classroom with step cards and materials.

Welcome: Classroom language 🙋

Salim says

Aim: Practise classroom instructions (Put up your hand, Tidy up…) through physical response.

Steps:

1. The teacher gives instructions; pupils follow them only when they start with Salim says.

2. A pupil who moves without Salim says loses a point.

3. Speed up the instructions, then let a pupil be the leader.

Welcome: Alphabet 🔤

The alphabet game & Spell it

Aim: Revise the alphabet and spelling (How do you spell …?).

Steps:

1. Each pupil says the next letter of the alphabet around the class.

2. In Spell it, pupils stand in a circle and each says one letter of the word.

3. If someone makes a mistake, the word starts again until it is spelt correctly.

Unit 1: Daily routines 🪰

Word Swat

Aim: Quickly recognise daily routine words.

Steps:

1. Stick the Unit 1 flashcards on the board and make two lines in front of it.

2. The leader of each line holds a fly swat; the teacher says a word and the fastest swats it.

3. The line that finishes first leaves the classroom first.

Unit 1: Daily routines 🎭

Mime game

Aim: Link daily routine verbs to actions and speaking.

Steps:

1. A pupil mimes a daily activity (get up, pray, have lunch) without speaking.

2. Classmates guess: You get up!

3. The pupil who guesses correctly mimes next.

Unit 1: Culture 🍳

Project: A breakfast menu in Oman

Aim: Design an Omani breakfast menu (Khubz Rakhal) using food vocabulary.

Steps:

1. The group thinks about what people like for breakfast in Oman.

2. Pupils draw the dishes and write their names in English.

3. Each group presents its menu to the class.

Unit 2: Clothes 🧢

What’s missing?

Aim: Practise clothes and personal possessions vocabulary.

Steps:

1. Stick the clothes flashcards on the board for one minute.

2. Pupils close their eyes and one card is removed.

3. Ask What’s missing? Pupils say the word.

Unit 2: English in action 🛍️

Class shop

Aim: Practise asking the price (How much is this …? It’s … OMR).

Steps:

1. Set up a shop corner with clothes, price tags and play money.

2. Pupils take turns being the shopkeeper and the customer using the full dialogue.

3. Focus on polite phrases: Excuse me / Thank you.

Unit 2: Culture 👗

Project: Design clothes for a special day

Aim: Design Eid clothes and describe them in English.

Steps:

1. Each pupil draws and colours traditional clothes for a special day.

2. Pupils write a description: She’s wearing traditional clothes.

3. Display the work and add it to portfolios.

Unit 3: Hobbies 🎾

Charades

Aim: Revise hobbies with the present simple (he/she).

Steps:

1. Pupils sit in a circle and pass the hobby cards while music plays.

2. When the music stops, the pupil holding the cards mimes a hobby.

3. Others guess: He plays tennis! She rides a bike!

Unit 3: Days of the week 📅

Snap!

Aim: Recognise and order the days of the week.

Steps:

1. The teacher says a day and shows the day cards one by one.

2. Pupils call Snap! when the matching card appears.

3. Extension: What day comes after …?

Unit 4: Jobs & transport 🃏

Memory game

Aim: Practise jobs and transport vocabulary.

Steps:

1. Each pair cuts a sheet into four pieces, draws four jobs/transport items and writes the words.

2. Cards are turned over and pupils try to remember the word on each card.

3. The pupil with the most cards wins.

Unit 4: Culture 📊

Project: How do you go to school? (graph)

Aim: Do a class survey and make a graph of how pupils go to school.

Steps:

1. Pupils ask each other: How do you go to school?

2. They count the pupils for each type of transport and draw a graph.

3. They write and present: In our group, four students go to school by car.
